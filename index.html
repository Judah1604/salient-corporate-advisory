Corporate Governance Advisory

Governance for growing businesses — not board secretarial services for listed companies.

Growing SMEs often reach a point where ownership and management blur, founder authority becomes excessive, decisions aren't clearly delegated, and controls stay informal.

Professional governance doesn't make a growing business bureaucratic. It makes decision-making clearer, accountability stronger, and the organisation less dependent on any one personality.

Delegation of Authority

Clear limits on who can decide what, at what value, without founder sign-off.

Decision Rights

Documented ownership of key decisions across the management layer.

Internal Controls

Systematic checks that reduce dependence on any single individual.

Governance Readiness

The oversight structures investors and institutional partners expect to see.

About

From founder-led to professionally managed.

Many businesses begin informally. The founder makes decisions, knows everyone, personally oversees important matters and solves problems as they arise.

But growth changes the equation. More people. More customers. More transactions. More managers. More risk. More decisions. Eventually, informal management becomes insufficient.

That is where Salient comes in. We help growing Nigerian businesses build the professional HR, organisational and governance infrastructure required to move from founder-dependent management to structured, accountable, sustainable operations.

Led by 24 years of professional experience across manufacturing, hospitality, engineering, food chains and multi-outlet operations in Nigeria: there is no bad organization — only bad management operating bad policies.
